Raqeem is a WhatsApp assistant that reminds your clients to send documents, collects them, checks them with AI and shows your team their status on a dashboard. We set up and run the service for you.

New firms can use Raqeem free for 30 days. No payment details are needed. At the end of the pilot you can choose a plan or stop. If you stop, we delete your data as described in our privacy policy.

You can cancel at any time. See our refund and cancellation policy for details.

Raqeem collects and checks documents. It does not give tax, legal or financial advice. AI can make mistakes, so your team stays responsible for reviewing documents and for all accounting and tax work.

  • تُطبّق رسوم إعداد لمرة واحدة ما لم يُعفَ منها بعرض مكتوب، مثل عرض المكاتب المؤسِّسة.
