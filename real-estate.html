Questions

Do I need a new phone number?

No. You connect the WhatsApp Business number you already use, and you can keep using the app on your phone. When you reply yourself, the AI steps back from that chat.

What about leads from Bayut and Property Finder?

Buyers who tap the WhatsApp button on your portal listings message your number, so the AI answers them like any other lead.

How do my listings get in?

Send us a spreadsheet or your listing links, and we load them during setup. Tell us when something sells or changes and we update it.

Which languages does it speak?

It replies in the language the buyer writes in, including English, Arabic, Hindi, Urdu, Russian and French.

Will buyers know it's AI?

It writes like a helpful assistant and says it's AI if asked. Viewings and offers are always handled by you.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time. You keep your WhatsApp number and your leads.
