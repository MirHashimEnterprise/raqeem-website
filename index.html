How it works

Your clients keep messaging the WhatsApp number they already know. Raqeem works quietly in the background.

1

Polite reminders

Each month, clients get a friendly WhatsApp message listing the documents your firm needs, with follow-ups if they forget.

2

AI checks every file

Raqeem reads each photo or PDF, labels it, and spots problems: blurry, cut off, wrong month, missing pages.

3

One clear list

Your team sees who has sent everything, who is still waiting, and which files need attention, all sorted and named.

Built for UAE firms

Made for the way small business clients in the UAE actually work.

  • Works on WhatsApp: no portal, no app, no login for your clients
  • Replies in English and Arabic, with Hindi and Urdu coming
  • Understands bank statements, invoices, receipts, trade licenses and IDs
  • Your team stays in control: staff confirm the AI's work before anything is filed
  • Raqeem collects and checks documents only. It does not give tax advice
