How it works

Your clients keep messaging a WhatsApp number with your firm's name. Raqeem works quietly behind it.

Step 1

Clients get a friendly reminder

Each month, every client gets a WhatsApp message listing exactly what your firm needs, with gentle follow-ups if they forget.

Step 2

They reply with photos or PDFs

Raqeem reads each file, labels it, and asks the client to resend anything blurry, cut off or from the wrong period.

Step 3

Your team sees one clear page

Who has sent everything, who is still waiting, and who needs a person. No more scrolling through hundreds of chats.

Questions firms ask us

Something else on your mind? Email raqeemhq@gmail.com.

Do my clients need to download an app?

No. They use the WhatsApp they already have. No links, no logins, no passwords. They just reply in the chat.

Which WhatsApp number will my clients message?

A dedicated business number that shows your firm's name. Your normal WhatsApp stays exactly as it is. We set up the new number for you during the pilot.

Is our clients' data safe?

Files are used only to collect and check them for your firm, never sold or shared, and deleted when you ask. We can sign a confidentiality agreement before you start.

Does Raqeem give tax or accounting advice?

No. Raqeem collects and checks documents. Questions about tax or bookkeeping are passed to your team, and your accountants stay in control of the work.

Which languages does it speak?

It replies in the language each client writes in, including English, Arabic, Hindi and Urdu.

What does it check in each document?

Whether it's readable, whether the whole page is there, whether it's the right month, whether it was already sent, and what kind of document it is. If something's wrong, it asks the client to fix it straight away.

What happens when a client asks something complicated?

Raqeem tells the client a team member will get back to them, and flags the chat on your dashboard so nothing gets missed.

How long does setup take, and what do you need from us?

Usually 2 to 3 days. We need your client list (names and WhatsApp numbers) and the documents you ask for each month. We do the rest.

What happens after the free month?

We show you what Raqeem handled during the pilot. If you want to continue, you pick a plan. If not, you walk away and we delete your data.

Can we cancel anytime?

Yes. Plans are billed monthly with no long contract. Cancel whenever you like.
